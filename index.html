Keep Count Android Mobile App

KeepCount is a fast, clean and distraction free counting app designed for focused repetition-ideal for Japa Mala, habit tracking, inventory or fitness sets. It’s built to be local-first ensuring your data is always private, secure and available instantly without requiring an account or internet connection.

🧘‍♂️ Core Counting Features

  • Customizable Cycles: Define a precise Cycle Size (eg: 108, 5, 1000) and track your progress through multiple cycles, giving structure to long sessions.

Flexible Input Methods:

  • Volume Key Control: Increase or decrease your count using the physical volume buttons. Perfect for counting in motion or when you do not wish to touch the screen.

  • On-Screen Tap: The traditional, large buttons on the screen to tap on.

  • Audio Feedback: Receive a satisfying, soothing Sound Alert upon completing a cycle providing non-visual confirmation without distracting your focus.

  • Keep Awake Mode: Enable the Screen Always On setting to prevent the screen from turning off during focused counting sessions.

🔒 Privacy & Optimized Experience

  • Private & Local-First: All data (coutners, settings, history_ is stored securely on your device. We handle zero user data.

  • 100% Free & Ad-Free: This app is and will always be completely free with absolutely no integrated ads.

  • Reliable Performance: Built on robust, asynchronous database to guarantee instant loading and seamless performance.

📊 Tracking & Management

  • Detailed Metrics: Easily track your Today’s Count (including your current cycle progress) and your total Lifetime Count.

  • Comprehensive History: View and manage a complete log of your counting activity over time. with detailed daily breakdowns.

  • Secure Maintenance: Reliably delete old counters. The app uses cascading deletion to ensure all associated history records are automatically and permanently removed, keeping your data clean.
